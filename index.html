Assessment

Four dimensions, four quarters.

Each piece of work is judged on four equal dimensions. The portfolio grows all year — the rubric is on the wall from Day 1.

CriterionWhat it rewardsWeight
Concept & CreativityOriginality of the character and its universe; how the character evolves across modules.25%
Technical SkillQuality of prompts, images, 3D, video, and the final website; tool fluency.25%
Iteration & ReflectionDesign-log entries; evidence of critique-and-iterate (the “filtering” skill).25%
PresentationFinal website polish + the 2-minute Week 34 showcase.25%
Privacy & Safety

Built-in, not bolted-on.

What we promise students and parents

  • Students appear on this hub by first initial and surname only. No full names, no student photos, no contact info.
  • This page asks search engines not to index it. It is shared by link, not published.
  • Student sites are share-with-link only — not public-indexed by search engines.
  • AI ethics is taught explicitly in Week 1 and revisited every module — hallucination, plagiarism, ownership.
  • Students treat AI output as a draft to filter, not a final answer — this is the core skill.
  • All tools used are school-licensed or education-tier, with verified age terms.
